## Reference documentation

From the reference LAPACK 3.12.0 source of `DLAEV2`, converted to Markdown with KaTeX math. Indices are the 1-based Fortran indices of the source.

> Computes the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a 2-by-2 symmetric/Hermitian matrix.

### Purpose

DLAEV2 computes the eigendecomposition of a 2-by-2 symmetric matrix

$$
\begin{bmatrix} A & B \\ B & C \end{bmatrix}
$$

On return, `RT1` is the eigenvalue of larger absolute value, `RT2` is the eigenvalue of smaller absolute value, and (`CS1`,`SN1`) is the unit right eigenvector for `RT1`, giving the decomposition

$$
\begin{bmatrix} \mathrm{CS1} & \mathrm{SN1} \\ -\mathrm{SN1} & \mathrm{CS1} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} A & B \\ B & C \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \mathrm{CS1} & -\mathrm{SN1} \\ \mathrm{SN1} & \mathrm{CS1} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \mathrm{RT1} & 0 \\ 0 & \mathrm{RT2} \end{bmatrix}
$$

### Arguments

#### A `[in]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

The (1,1) element of the 2-by-2 matrix.

#### B `[in]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

The (1,2) element and the conjugate of the (2,1) element of the 2-by-2 matrix.

#### C `[in]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

The (2,2) element of the 2-by-2 matrix.

#### RT1 `[out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

The eigenvalue of larger absolute value.

#### RT2 `[out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

The eigenvalue of smaller absolute value.

#### CS1 `[out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

#### SN1 `[out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

The vector (`CS1`, `SN1`) is a unit right eigenvector for `RT1`.

### Further Details

`RT1` is accurate to a few ulps barring over/underflow.

`RT2` may be inaccurate if there is massive cancellation in the determinant $A \, C-B \, B$; higher precision or correctly rounded or correctly truncated arithmetic would be needed to compute `RT2` accurately in all cases.

`CS1` and `SN1` are accurate to a few ulps barring over/underflow.

Overflow is possible only if `RT1` is within a factor of 5 of overflow. Underflow is harmless if the input data is 0 or exceeds

underflow\_threshold / macheps.
