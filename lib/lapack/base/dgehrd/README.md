# dgehrd

> Reduces a real general matrix to upper Hessenberg form using blocked algorithm.

<section class="usage">

## Usage

```javascript
var dgehrd = require( '@stdlib/lapack/base/dgehrd' );
```

#### dgehrd( order, N, ilo, ihi, A, LDA, TAU, strideTAU, WORK, strideWORK )

Reduces a real general matrix to upper Hessenberg form using blocked algorithm.

```javascript
var dgehrd = require( '@stdlib/lapack/base/dgehrd' );

var N = 3;
var A = discreteUniform( N * N, -10, 10, opts );
var TAU = discreteUniform( N, -10, 10, opts );
var WORK = discreteUniform( N, -10, 10, opts );

dgehrd.ndarray( N, N, N, A, N, 1, 0, TAU, 1, 0, WORK, 1, 0 );
```

The function has the following parameters:

-   **order**: storage layout (`'row-major'` or `'column-major'`).
-   **N**: number of columns.
-   **ilo**: `ilo`.
-   **ihi**: `ihi`.
-   **A**: input array `A`.
-   **LDA**: leading dimension of `A`.
-   **TAU**: input array `TAU`.
-   **strideTAU**: stride length for `TAU`.
-   **WORK**: input array `WORK`.
-   **strideWORK**: stride length for `WORK`.

#### dgehrd.ndarray( N, ilo, ihi, A, strideA1, strideA2, offsetA, TAU, strideTAU, offsetTAU, WORK, strideWORK, offsetWORK )

Reduces a real general matrix to upper Hessenberg form using blocked algorithm, using alternative indexing semantics.

```javascript
var dgehrd = require( '@stdlib/lapack/base/dgehrd' );

var N = 3;
var A = discreteUniform( N * N, -10, 10, opts );
var TAU = discreteUniform( N, -10, 10, opts );
var WORK = discreteUniform( N, -10, 10, opts );

dgehrd.ndarray( N, N, N, A, N, 1, 0, TAU, 1, 0, WORK, 1, 0 );
```

The function has the following additional parameters:

-   **strideA1**: stride of dimension 1 of `A`.
-   **strideA2**: stride of dimension 2 of `A`.
-   **offsetA**: starting index for `A`.
-   **offsetTAU**: starting index for `TAU`.
-   **offsetWORK**: starting index for `WORK`.

## Reference documentation

From the reference LAPACK 3.12.0 source of `DGEHRD`, converted to Markdown with KaTeX math. Indices are the 1-based Fortran indices of the source.

### Purpose

DGEHRD reduces a real general matrix A to upper Hessenberg form H by an orthogonal similarity transformation: $Q^T \, A \, Q = H$ .

### Arguments

#### N `[in]`

_INTEGER_

The order of the matrix A. $N \ge 0$.

#### ILO `[in]`

_INTEGER_

#### IHI `[in]`

_INTEGER_

It is assumed that A is already upper triangular in rows and columns 1:`ILO`-1 and `IHI`+1:N. `ILO` and `IHI` are normally set by a previous call to DGEBAL; otherwise they should be set to 1 and N respectively. See Further Details. $1 \le \mathrm{ILO} \le \mathrm{IHI} \le N$, if $N > 0$; `ILO=1` and `IHI=0`, if $N=0$.

#### A `[in,out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION array, dimension (LDA,N)_

On entry, the N-by-N general matrix to be reduced. On exit, the upper triangle and the first subdiagonal of A are overwritten with the upper Hessenberg matrix H, and the elements below the first subdiagonal, with the array `TAU`, represent the orthogonal matrix Q as a product of elementary reflectors. See Further Details.

#### LDA `[in]`

_INTEGER_

The leading dimension of the array A. $\mathrm{LDA} \ge \max (1,N)$.

#### TAU `[out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION array, dimension (N-1)_

The scalar factors of the elementary reflectors (see Further Details). Elements 1:`ILO`-1 and `IHI`:N-1 of `TAU` are set to zero.

#### WORK `[out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION array, dimension (LWORK)_

On exit, if `INFO = 0`, `WORK(1)` returns the optimal `LWORK`.

#### LWORK `[in]`

_INTEGER_

The length of the array `WORK`. $\mathrm{LWORK} \ge \max (1,N)$. For good performance, `LWORK` should generally be larger.

If `LWORK = -1`, then a workspace query is assumed; the routine only calculates the optimal size of the `WORK` array, returns this value as the first entry of the `WORK` array, and no error message related to `LWORK` is issued by XERBLA.

#### INFO `[out]`

_INTEGER_

-   `= 0`: successful exit
-   `< 0`: if `INFO = -i`, the i-th argument had an illegal value.

### Further Details

The matrix Q is represented as a product of (ihi-ilo) elementary reflectors

Q = H(ilo) H(ilo+1) . . . H(ihi-1).

Each H(i) has the form

$$
H(i) = I - \tau \, v \, v^T
$$

where $\tau$ is a real scalar, and v is a real vector with $v(1:i) = 0$, $v(i+1) = 1$ and v(ihi+1:n) = 0; v(i+2:ihi) is stored on exit in `A(i+2:ihi,i)`, and $\tau$ in `TAU(i)`.

The contents of A are illustrated by the following example, with $n = 7$, ilo = 2 and ihi = 6:

```text
on entry,                        on exit,
```

$$
\begin{pmatrix} a & a & a & a & a & a & a \\  & a & a & a & a & a & a \\  & a & a & a & a & a & a \\  & a & a & a & a & a & a \\  & a & a & a & a & a & a \\  & a & a & a & a & a & a \\  &  &  &  &  &  & a \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} a & a & h & h & h & h & a \\  & a & h & h & h & h & a \\  & h & h & h & h & h & h \\  & v_{2} & h & h & h & h & h \\  & v_{2} & v_{3} & h & h & h & h \\  & v_{2} & v_{3} & v_{4} & h & h & h \\  &  &  &  &  &  & a \end{pmatrix}
$$

where a denotes an element of the original matrix A, h denotes a modified element of the upper Hessenberg matrix H, and vi denotes an element of the vector defining H(i).

This file is a slight modification of LAPACK-3.0's DGEHRD subroutine incorporating improvements proposed by Quintana-Orti and Van de Geijn (2006). (See DLAHR2.)
