## Reference notes

The mathematical description from the reference LAPACK 3.12.0 source of `DLANV2`. Indices are the 1-based Fortran indices of the source.

### Purpose

DLANV2 computes the Schur factorization of a real 2-by-2 nonsymmetric matrix in standard form:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \mathrm{CS} & -\mathrm{SN} \\ \mathrm{SN} & \mathrm{CS} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \mathrm{AA} & \mathrm{BB} \\ \mathrm{CC} & \mathrm{DD} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \mathrm{CS} & \mathrm{SN} \\ -\mathrm{SN} & \mathrm{CS} \end{bmatrix}
$$

where either 1) $\mathrm{CC} = 0$ so that AA and DD are real eigenvalues of the matrix, or 2) $\mathrm{AA} = \mathrm{DD}$ and $\mathrm{BB} \, \mathrm{CC} < 0$, so that AA + or - sqrt(BB\*CC) are complex conjugate eigenvalues.

### Further Details

Modified by V. Sima, Research Institute for Informatics, Bucharest, Romania, to reduce the risk of cancellation errors, when computing real eigenvalues, and to ensure, if possible, that $\lvert \mathrm{RT1R} \rvert \ge \lvert \mathrm{RT2R} \rvert$.
