## Reference documentation

From the reference LAPACK 3.12.0 source of `DLASV2`, converted to Markdown with KaTeX math. Indices are the 1-based Fortran indices of the source.

> Computes the singular value decomposition of a 2-by-2 triangular matrix.

### Purpose

DLASV2 computes the singular value decomposition of a 2-by-2 triangular matrix

$$
\begin{bmatrix} F & G \\ 0 & H \end{bmatrix}
$$

On return, abs(`SSMAX`) is the larger singular value, abs(`SSMIN`) is the smaller singular value, and (`CSL`,`SNL`) and (`CSR`,`SNR`) are the left and right singular vectors for abs(`SSMAX`), giving the decomposition

$$
\begin{bmatrix} \mathrm{CSL} & \mathrm{SNL} \\ -\mathrm{SNL} & \mathrm{CSL} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} F & G \\ 0 & H \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \mathrm{CSR} & -\mathrm{SNR} \\ \mathrm{SNR} & \mathrm{CSR} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \mathrm{SSMAX} & 0 \\ 0 & \mathrm{SSMIN} \end{bmatrix}
$$

### Arguments

#### F `[in]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

The (1,1) element of the 2-by-2 matrix.

#### G `[in]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

The (1,2) element of the 2-by-2 matrix.

#### H `[in]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

The (2,2) element of the 2-by-2 matrix.

#### SSMIN `[out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

abs(`SSMIN`) is the smaller singular value.

#### SSMAX `[out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

abs(`SSMAX`) is the larger singular value.

#### SNL `[out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

#### CSL `[out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

The vector (`CSL`, `SNL`) is a unit left singular vector for the singular value abs(`SSMAX`).

#### SNR `[out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

#### CSR `[out]`

_DOUBLE PRECISION_

The vector (`CSR`, `SNR`) is a unit right singular vector for the singular value abs(`SSMAX`).

### Further Details

Any input parameter may be aliased with any output parameter.

Barring over/underflow and assuming a guard digit in subtraction, all output quantities are correct to within a few units in the last place (ulps).

In IEEE arithmetic, the code works correctly if one matrix element is infinite.

Overflow will not occur unless the largest singular value itself overflows or is within a few ulps of overflow.

Underflow is harmless if underflow is gradual. Otherwise, results may correspond to a matrix modified by perturbations of size near the underflow threshold.
